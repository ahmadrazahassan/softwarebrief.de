Background: genuine transparent alpha background.
Constraints: clean crisp vector-like edges in a high-resolution PNG; simple flat shapes; no stock envelope outline, no generic cube or circuit icon, no hexagon, no imitation of Sage or another brand, no 3D, no shadows, no mockup, no decorative border, no watermark.
